## Must fix (for the `frontend` agent)

1. **Turn off MFA is offered to admins, and the engine refuses it.** `/me/security` shows "Turn off MFA…" to every user with MFA on; for admins with "Require MFA for admins" on (the default) the engine answers 409 "MFA is required for admins." after the user has typed their password. This breaks the rule in [information-architecture.md](../information-architecture.md#role-aware-visibility): never a button that looks clickable and then fails. Fix: when the user is an admin and MFA is required for admins, replace the button with the text "MFA is required for admins." (needs the flag from `GET /auth/me`, or treat admins as required until Phase 15 adds the setting).
2. **No way back from the second step.** `/login/mfa`, `/login/recovery` and the forced `/mfa/enrol` have no "Back to sign in" or "Sign out". A user without their phone, or a new admin who can't enrol right now, is held in a partial session with no visible exit except editing the URL. Fix: one link on all three, "Sign in as someone else", that posts `/auth/logout` and goes to `/login`. On `/login/recovery` add one line under the form: "Lost your authenticator and your codes? Ask an admin to reset your MFA."
3. **Times have no time zone and no relative form.** `when()` in `web/lib/utils.ts` prints `11/19/2026, 1:05:00 PM` in the browser's locale with no zone. On the audit log (evidence an auditor compares with Splunk and firewall logs), on sessions and on token expiry this is ambiguous. [copy.md](../copy.md#voice) says relative with the absolute time on hover and in the accessible name, zone named. Fix once in `when()`: render "5 minutes ago" with `<time dateTime title>` carrying `2026-11-19 13:05 UTC` (or the local zone, named); the audit log may show the absolute form in the cell, but with the zone.

| # | Deviation | Severity |

| L2 | "Your session ended." lacks the why from [copy.md](../copy.md#error-pattern) ("You were inactive for 30 minutes."). The client can't always know the reason (revoked vs idle); acceptable until the idle banner exists. | later (with the idle banner) |
